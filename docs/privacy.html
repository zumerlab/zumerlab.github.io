#Privacy

>zumerlab.com uses Google Analytics to see which pages, docs and demos people use, so we know what to improve. This page covers the whole site: the home page, Orbit, Orbit Kit and Zumly.

##What is measured

  • -The pages you visit and how you got here: a link, a search engine, another site.
  • -What you do on them: the links and buttons you click, the demos you try, code you edit or copy in an example, how far you scroll.
  • -Your approximate location (country and city) and your browser and device, as Google Analytics reports them.

>Nothing that names you: no forms, no accounts, no email addresses. There are no ads, and the data is not used to track you on other sites.

##Cookies and retention

  • -Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_XL52CBETDH. They tell one browser from another and say nothing about who you are.
  • -Detailed event data is kept for 2 months and data tied to a browser for 14 months. After that, only totals remain.
  • -Google processes the data for us. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

##Your choice

  • -In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, no analytics cookies are set until you accept the cookie banner. If you decline, nothing is stored.
  • -Elsewhere, analytics runs from the first visit, and you can turn it off here.
  • -Your choice is saved in this browser and applies to every page of zumerlab.com. Declining also deletes the cookies above.
